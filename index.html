The difference

A reasoning engine, not a chart generator.

Most tools hand you a canvas and make you go looking for the story. Ellery does the reasoning first, states what it found in plain language, and treats visualizations as lenses onto the same conclusions.

01 · Findings first

It opens with the answer

Load data and Ellery presents a ranked Case File — the important findings, in plain English, before you touch a single chart.

02 · The Pull

Every finding is provable

Click a finding and follow it down through its evidence, derivation, policy and confidence to the originating cells. Reasoning you can inspect, not just read.

03 · Deterministic

Same data, same result

No model in the truth path. Findings are a pure function of evidence and policy, fingerprinted to a snapshot — reproducible by construction.
