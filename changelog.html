Changelog

What's new.

Ellery is in early access. Progress is shipped in small, deliberate steps — the reasoning engine first, then the product around it.

Preview v1Jul 2026

The Pull reaches fingerprinted ground interaction

  • Any finding now traces down through evidence, derivation, policy, and confidence.
  • Records open to their raw cells, each with its own content fingerprint.
  • The trace terminates at the snapshot fingerprint — reproducible ground, not a highlight.
  • A traced finding stays lit across every lens.
v0.4Jul 2026

Findings-first workspace workflow

  • The app now opens on the Case File — findings before charts.
  • Visualizations became lenses onto one shared understanding, switched from a lens bar.
  • The Discovery Engine surfaces for free on every dataset.
v0.3Jul 2026

Interface refresh design

  • Quieter, denser, more intentional — hierarchy through typography and spacing, not chrome.
  • Clear primary actions; secondary actions recede.
v0.2Jun 2026

Deterministic reasoning engines engine

  • Discovery, Relationship, and Decision engines — standalone, immutable, dependency-free.
  • Every finding carries structured evidence and a stable identity.
v0.1Jun 2026

First light foundation

  • Ingestion for CSV, TSV, JSON, and text.
  • Kinetic, Timeline, Cards, and Mindmap layouts with presentation-ready exports.
